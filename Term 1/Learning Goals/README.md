You write **three** learning goals. Each one gets its own file:

- [Learning_Goal_1.md](Learning_Goal_1.md)
- [Learning_Goal_2.md](Learning_Goal_2.md)
- [Learning_Goal_3.md](Learning_Goal_3.md)

See [GANTT_CHART_GUIDE.md](GANTT_CHART_GUIDE.md) for how to make your timeline chart.

---

## What each plan must answer

| Question | What we are looking for |
|---|---|
| **What will you achieve?** | State the goal clearly and concretely. What skill, output or milestone? |
| **Why do you want to achieve it?** | Explain why this learning goal is of value **to you**. |
| **How will you achieve it?** | What steps, activities or methods will you use? |
| **Who or what do you need?** | People (peers, instructors, domain experts), tools, data, or resources required. |
| **Timeline** | When do you start, what are the milestones, when do you expect to finish? It is best if you make a **Gantt chart** to visualise your timeline. |

---

## How to choose good goals

- Connect them to **the minor** and to your **Big Project direction in Term 2**.
- But make sure they are of value **to you specifically**.
- Pick goals you wanted to learn about during this minor **that we did not provide**.
- **Be specific.** Vague goals are hard to assess and harder to achieve.

### Vague vs. specific

| Vague | Specific |
|---|---|
| "Get better at AI" | "Fine-tune a small open-source language model on Dutch municipal FAQ data and measure whether it beats a plain prompt on 50 test questions" |
| "Learn about data" | "Build and publish an interactive dashboard of The Hague's air-quality data that updates daily from an open API" |
| "Improve my presenting" | "Present my Big Project three times to non-technical audiences, collect written feedback each time, and rewrite the deck between rounds" |
| "Do something with ethics" | "Run a bias audit on my Term 2 model using a documented method, and write up which groups it underperforms for" |

**Test your goal:** could someone else look at your portfolio in Week 20 and say *yes, you did that* or *no, you did not*? If not, it is still too vague.
