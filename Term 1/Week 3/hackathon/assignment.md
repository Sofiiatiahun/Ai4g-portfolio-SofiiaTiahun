## The challenge

Use an LLM API (via Python) to build a tool that reduces a specific inequality. The solution must make real API calls, handle responses, and deliver something a real person could use.

Examples: a language accessibility tool, a legal or medical information explainer for non-experts, a bias checker for job postings, a tool that bridges information gaps for underrepresented groups.

## Requirements

1. A Python-native script or app that makes live API calls to Claude or Gemini.
2. Addresses a concrete, specific inequality
3. Handles edge cases: what happens when the AI gives a bad answer?
4. Includes the ethical reflection below

## Ethical reflection (required)

What are the risks of your tool? Who could it harm? A tool meant to reduce inequality can create new ones — wrong advice to people who cannot verify it, or better service for some languages than others. Name the biggest risk and how you limit it.

*Include your answer in your submission — a short paragraph is enough, but it must be honest and specific to your project. **Remember 1st weeks feedback, we want to see real numbers backed by solid sources.***

## What to submit

1. A link to (or zip of) the working product
2. A README: what it does, who it is for, which SDG it addresses and why, and how to run it
3. A small demo of the working product (e.g. a screen recording of you using it)
4. Your ethical reflection (see above)
