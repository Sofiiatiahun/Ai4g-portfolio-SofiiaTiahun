## How to use these folders

- Add your work each week - code, notes, designs, data, screenshots.
- Fill in the `README.md` in each week folder. It takes two minutes, and it is what makes your
  portfolio readable.
- **Keep your learning goals alive.** Every week folder asks which of your three goals you moved
  forward. Go back to [`Term 1/Learning Goals/`](../Term%201/Learning%20Goals/) and update the
  progress log and the Gantt chart there.

> Your instructor will confirm the exact Term 2 deliverables and deadlines. Until then, treat every
> week as: *build something, write down what you learned, commit it.*
